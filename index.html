hyperquest

Complex language. Simple extraction patterns. Structured knowledge.

HYPERQUEST turns unstructured text into structured knowledge.

It extracts information from large text collections using linguistic patterns that are easy to define, yet expressive enough to capture the richness of natural language. The extracted knowledge can then be explored through semantic graphs, stored in relational databases, or provided to LLMs as structured context for downstream extraction and reasoning.

extracting the pattern

Consider a corpus of press headlines. We want to focus on sentences where one actor accuses another of something — for instance:

"Turkey accuses Russia of ethnic cleansing"

In a semantic hypergraph, this maps to a structured hyperedge representation. Crucially, it lets us define the pattern x accuses y spec to sweep an entire corpus and retrieve all matching headlines at once.
